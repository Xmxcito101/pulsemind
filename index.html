Who We Are

Nigeria's intelligence layer for the 2027 election

// Platform Statement

PulseMind is the real-time sentiment intelligence platform that transforms unstructured online conversations into strategic decisions — telling you not just what people think, but who is thinking it, how loudly, and exactly what to do next.

// Problem We Solve

Every day, billions of opinions are published online — yet campaigns and brands make critical decisions based on instinct or outdated reports. They can't see what's happening to their reputation in real time, who's driving the narrative, or whether their response is working.

// Our Solution

PulseMind monitors 40+ platforms — X/Twitter, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Reddit, YouTube, Nigerian news sites and more — applying AI-powered multilingual sentiment analysis to every mention of your candidate, brand, or topic. You get live dashboards, top account attribution, geographic breakdowns by state and geopolitical zone, and AI-generated action plans — from data to decision in one screen.
